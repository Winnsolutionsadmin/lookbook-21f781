Travel Lookbook

New York · Seoul · Singapore. One uniform, bought once, worn every day. Everything below is chosen for a man who overheats and needs to read as the most successful person in the room without thinking about it.

The look

Meeting
Navy unlined jacket, grey trousers, white or light-blue shirt, suede loafers. Jacket goes on at the door.
Dinner / walk-around
Same jacket over a navy knit polo. No tie, no stiffness, still expensive-looking. Cooler than a buttoned shirt.
Retire the black shirt. Black absorbs heat and reads "nightlife," not "executive." Navy, charcoal, grey, white, light blue only. The Rolex does the rest.

City rules

New York. The uniform is the default. No tie. Jacket + polo is fine for every dinner.
Seoul. Most conservative stop. Executives are impeccably groomed and fitted · a wrinkled shirt costs you more here than anywhere. Jacket into every meeting, shirt over polo for corporate/bank/government, and pack one navy tie for that scenario. Loafers are fine.
Singapore. 32°C / 80% humidity outside, 19°C inside. Nobody wears a jacket on the street; everyone carries one and puts it on in the conference room. Long-sleeve shirt, sleeves down, is the local business norm. This is where tropical wool and the AIRism layer earn their keep.
