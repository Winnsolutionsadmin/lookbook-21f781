One Layer. Short Sleeves. Still Rich.

Version 3, for a man who overheats. Nothing on this page is long-sleeved, nothing goes over anything else, and nothing needs an undershirt. One expensive short-sleeve top, lightweight dark jeans, Zegna sneakers, the Rolex. That is the actual uniform of wealthy men in Singapore and it is exactly one layer. Why one layer still reads as money. The tell is the fabric, the fit and the shoes, not the number of layers. A silk-cotton or Sea Island tee that hangs right, dark clean denim hemmed to the shoe, suede Zegna sneakers, a Rolex: that outfit costs more than a suit and everyone who matters can see it. A crisp collar (polo) is the only thing you need for a meeting; Singapore business casual is a polo, full stop.

The formula

Meetings
Knit or piqué polo (collar), lightweight dark jeans, Triple Stitch, watch.
Everything else
Silk-cotton tee, same jeans, same shoes. Evening: swap to the white one.

Rules for the trip

No undershirt. You said no layers; the tops above are dark or white and none of them show sweat the way a pale blue shirt does. If you change your mind, one Uniqlo AIRism under a polo is invisible and cooler than skin against piqué.
Seoul. A collared polo for every meeting; the Cucinelli or the Riviera. A silk-cotton tee is fine for dinner. Nobody expects a foreign visitor in a jacket in September heat, and a Rolex over a $500 polo is read correctly there.
Singapore. Polo or tee, jeans, Triple Stitch, every day. Every building is 19°C inside; if that bothers you it bothers you for twenty minutes, then you are back outside at 32°C. Do not carry a jacket for it.
Buy there: Zegna Oasi Lino polo (L) and any Loro Piana polo at Cheongdam (Seoul) or Marina Bay Sands (Singapore). VAT and GST come back at the airport.
