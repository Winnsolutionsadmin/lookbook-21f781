Expensive Jeans, Expensive Top

Version 2. Not preppy, not a suit. Dark Italian denim, one shirt or knit that costs more than most people's jackets, and a soft blazer or overshirt for the room that needs it. Every piece is from a house that people who have money recognize on sight, none of it has a logo, and all of it breathes. Version 1 (tailoring) is here.

The formula

Day
Dark jeans, Cucinelli knit polo or Zegna linen shirt, Zegna sneakers, watch. That is the whole outfit.
Meeting / dinner
Same jeans and shirt, add the Zegna silk-linen overshirt or the navy blazer. Take it off when it's hot; the shirt under it still reads rich.
Why this reads "success" and the black Boss shirt doesn't. Rich-casual is about fabric and cut, not color. Cucinelli, Zegna and Loro Piana cost 5 to 10x Boss and it shows at three feet: the knit has weight, the linen has slub, the denim is dark and clean with no distressing. Dark indigo or navy jeans, never black, never faded. Fit slim through the leg, hem just touching the shoe.

Where to buy in person

  • New York: Zegna 5th Ave and Cucinelli Madison Ave for fittings; Bergdorf men's (5th Ave) has Jacob Cohën, Tom Ford, Cucinelli, Zegna and Loro Piana on one floor, with a free personal shopper who will do this in one appointment.
  • Seoul: Zegna and Cucinelli in Cheongdam; Galleria and Shinsegae Gangnam carry all of it.
  • Singapore: Marina Bay Sands Shoppes has Zegna, Cucinelli and Loro Piana within a hundred metres of each other.
